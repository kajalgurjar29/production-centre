2. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small data files stored on a visitor’s device when a website is used.

They can support essential website functions, remember selected preferences and help provide a consistent website experience.

3. Cookies Used for the MVP

The MVP website may use cookies that are necessary for:

  • Website security
  • Core website operation
  • Remembering cookie choices
  • Maintaining essential user interactions

Essential cookies may operate because they are required for the website to function properly.

Details of individual cookies, providers and retention periods will be added when confirmed.

4. Optional Cookies

Optional cookies will be used only where they are introduced into the website and the visitor has made an appropriate choice.

Optional cookies may support functions such as website measurement or enhanced features.

Do not state that analytics, advertising or marketing cookies are currently used unless their implementation has been separately confirmed.

5. Managing Cookie Choices

Visitors should be able to manage optional cookie choices through the website cookie controls.

The controls should provide options to:

  • Accept optional cookies
  • Reject optional cookies
  • Review or change an earlier choice

Essential cookies will remain active where they are required for core website operation.

6. Browser Controls

Visitors may also manage cookies through their browser settings.

Changing browser settings may affect the operation of some website functions.

Do not include browser-specific instructions or external browser links in this MVP page.

7. Third-Party Services

Some website features may be provided through third-party services.

Where a third-party service sets cookies, the relevant information should be added to this policy only after the provider and cookie details have been confirmed.

Do not name any third-party provider in the current MVP Cookie Policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated when:

  • Website features change
  • New cookies are introduced
  • Existing cookies are removed
  • Legal or regulatory requirements change

The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.
